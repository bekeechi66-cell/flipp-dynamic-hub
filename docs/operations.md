# ⚙️ Flipp Dynamic Hub - Operations Manual

## Operations Overview

This document outlines the operational procedures and processes for running Flipp Dynamic Hub efficiently and professionally.

## 1. Inventory Management

### Stock Levels

#### Minimum Stock Policy
- Always maintain 20+ units per popular tyre model
- Monitor slow-moving inventory monthly
- Seasonal adjustments based on demand forecasts

#### Reordering Process
1. Monitor inventory levels (automated alerts at 15% stock)
2. Generate purchase orders from approved suppliers
3. Track shipments and delivery dates
4. Verify received stock against order
5. Update inventory system
6. Process payments to suppliers

### Warehouse Operations

#### Receiving
- Inspect incoming shipments for damage
- Verify against purchase orders
- Scan and catalog tyres
- Store in designated zones
- Update inventory system

#### Storage
- Climate-controlled warehouse (15-25°C)
- Organized by brand, size, and type
- Clear labeling and signage
- Regular inventory audits (weekly)
- Damage prevention protocols

#### Picking & Packing
- Order received from customer
- Locate tyres in warehouse
- Quality check before packing
- Secure packaging to prevent damage
- Generate shipping labels
- Ready for dispatch

## 2. Customer Order Processing

### Order Workflow

```
Customer Order → Payment Processing → Confirmation Email
       ↓
   Inventory Check → Picking & Packing → Quality Review
       ↓
   Shipping Label → Dispatch → Tracking Update
       ↓
   Delivery → Installation (if booked) → Customer Feedback
```

### Payment Processing

**Accepted Payment Methods:**
- Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Digital Wallets (PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay)
- Bank Transfers

**Payment Verification:**

- Invoice generation

### Order Confirmation

### Service Booking

**Available Services:**
- Tyre installation (₹200-300 per tyre)
- Wheel balancing (₹150-200 per wheel)
